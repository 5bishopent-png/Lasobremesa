The character of the meal begins long before the first course. In the depth of a mole, the tenderness of a slow-cooked dish, and a tortilla made to be shared.

Mexican cooking is at the heart of Sobre Mesa, informed by international technique and more than twelve years of life in Mexico.

The person behind the table

Chef Adam Bishop

A culinary point of view formed through more than twelve years in Mexico.

Life in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico City and Jalisco shaped Adam’s understanding of ingredients, regional cooking and the hospitality of a shared table. Formal Franco-Mexican culinary education brings a foundation of technique to that lived experience.

At Sobre Mesa, he brings those influences together in private dining and culinary events: personal, generous and grounded in the people being served.
