The person behind the table

Chef Adam Bishop

A culinary point of view formed through more than twelve years in Mexico.

Life in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico City and Jalisco shaped Adam’s understanding of ingredients, regional cooking and the hospitality of a shared table. Formal Franco-Mexican training brings a foundation of technique to that lived experience.

At Sobre Mesa, he brings those influences together in private dining and culinary events: personal, generous and grounded in the people being served.
